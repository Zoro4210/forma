03 / Product notesSunday, from the inside out

Made to
settle into.

Proportions, construction,
and a little everyday care.

W 298 × D 168 × H 78 cm

The Sunday chaise measures 298 cm wide × 168 cm deep × 78 cm high. A 44 cm seat height and 68 cm seat depth make space to settle in. Allow at least 80 cm around your sofa for an easy walkway.

A supportive timber frame gives Sunday its structure. Layered foam cushions soften the landing, while generously rounded arms make every corner a comfortable one. Modules connect beneath the seat to keep the silhouette clean.

Vacuum upholstery gently with a soft brush attachment. Blot spills promptly with a clean, dry cloth and avoid rubbing. Keep your sofa away from prolonged direct sunlight, and rotate loose cushions regularly for even wear.
